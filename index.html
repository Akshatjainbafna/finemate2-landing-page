You match on what
you both actually want.

Set your intent once. Swipe freely. Match only when both sides align. No guessing, no leading anyone on, no wasted conversations.

01

Long-press the heart button to pick what you're genuinely looking for. It stays set across every swipe until you change it yourself. No pop-up on every card. No friction. The dopamine stays intact.

Got questions

Everything you need
to know about Finemate.

What is Finemate?
Finemate is India's first communication-first dating app. Unlike Tinder or Bumble, Finemate shows you a 3D avatar based on someone's real face — personality lands before photos. It matches people based on communication patterns, not looks, and guarantees a 50:50 male-to-female ratio so the platform is actually enjoyable for everyone.
Is Finemate the best dating app in India?
Finemate is built specifically for India — addressing problems no other dating app has solved. It guarantees a 50:50 gender ratio by design, uses avatar-first discovery (so your face isn't the first thing people judge), matches you by how you actually communicate (not a bio you wrote), and lets you set per-match intentions so there's no ambiguity. Early users rank it 4.8/5 in satisfaction.
How is Finemate different from Tinder or Bumble?
Three fundamental differences: (1) Avatar-first identity — you see a 3D avatar before photos, so personality comes first. (2) Communication-pattern matching — your profile is derived from how you actually communicate (reply speed, message depth, humour style), not a bio you wrote. (3) Guaranteed 50:50 gender ratio — unlike Tinder's 9:1 male skew, Finemate enforces gender balance by design and women control when photos are revealed.
Can I find a girlfriend or boyfriend on Finemate?
Yes. Finemate is designed for meaningful connections — whether you're looking for a girlfriend, boyfriend, long-term relationship, or just someone to talk to. Every match includes a per-match intention setting (Long-term, Casual, Marriage, ENM, and more) so there is never any ambiguity about what someone wants from a connection.
Is Finemate safe for women?
Safety for women is built into Finemate's core architecture. Women control when photos are revealed — enforced at the server level, not just the UI. The guaranteed 50:50 gender ratio means women aren't overwhelmed by messages. Communication-pattern matching filters out bad actors who rely on surface-level tactics. Selfie verification ensures no fake profiles.
Which cities is Finemate launching in?
Finemate is launching first in Mumbai, Delhi, and Bangalore. Hyderabad, Pune, and Chennai are next in the rollout. Join the waitlist to get priority access and founding-member status when we launch in your city. Over 400+ people are already on the list.
How does Finemate match people?
Finemate matches using communication patterns — your reply speed, message depth, humour style, and topic preference. A 20-question onboarding questionnaire defines your initial profile, which refines over time from real in-app behaviour. You receive 10 curated matches every day at 9 AM. You cannot fake your communication style because you didn't consciously declare it.
Is Finemate free to use?
Yes — Finemate is free to join. Early access members who sign up at inemate.club get priority queue position and founding-member status when we launch. No App Store listing yet — this is true early access, and your waitlist position improves when you refer friends.
I just want someone to talk to — is Finemate right for me?
Absolutely. Finemate is a social-first app — dating is optional. You can browse the social feed, engage with posts, and have conversations without ever flipping into dating mode. The communication-first design means you connect with people who genuinely share how you think and talk, making it ideal for finding meaningful conversations, not just dates.
